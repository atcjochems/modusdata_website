Personal data we may process

When you contact us, subscribe to a service, or engage in a business relationship, we may process personal data such as your name, company, email address, phone number, communication history, and project-related information necessary for the purpose of the engagement.

Purposes of processing

We process personal data for the following purposes:

  • responding to inquiries and requests
  • managing client and project relationships
  • providing consulting, engineering, and AI implementation services
  • fulfilling legal, accounting, and contractual obligations
  • maintaining the security and integrity of our website and systems

Legal basis

We process personal data on the basis of legitimate interest, contract performance, compliance with legal obligations, and, where applicable, your consent. In some cases, Swiss and European data protection rules may also require us to provide additional information or to honor specific rights.

Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the stated purpose, legal obligations, or the duration of a contractual relationship. Once the need has passed, we delete or anonymize the data unless retention is required by law.

Your rights

Depending on the applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • request access to your personal data
  • request correction or deletion
  • object to certain processing activities
  • request restriction of processing
  • request data portability where technically feasible
  • withdraw consent where consent was used as the legal basis

If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at arthur@modusdata.ch.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party tracking for profiling. If we introduce analytics or marketing technologies in the future, we will update this policy and provide the corresponding notice and consent mechanisms where required.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect operational changes or legal requirements. The latest version will always be published on this page.
